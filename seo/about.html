About ORBIDENSE

ORBIDENSE is an independent environmental and climate-data platform designed to make complex Earth-system information easier to explore, compare and interpret.

Purpose

The platform connects environmental observations, climate information, emissions, population exposure and geospatial context in an interactive research-oriented environment.

Scientific direction

ORBIDENSE is being developed around evidence-based environmental analysis, transparent data provenance, reproducible methods and progressively deeper climate-risk research.

Current focus

The platform currently emphasizes climate and environmental intelligence, with future research development focused especially on urban climate risk, extreme heat, vulnerability and adaptation.
